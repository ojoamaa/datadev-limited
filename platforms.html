SIX CORE PLATFORMS

Six sectors. Six core platforms. One shared digital architecture.

DataDev concentrates product development on agriculture, human services, hospitality, health, energy and education. The platforms may be at different maturity levels, but each is treated as a serious sector solution with its own pathway to pilot, partnership and production.

AGRICULTURE · ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

AGROW

Agricultural field intelligence and programme operations connecting farmer registration, digital identity, GIS/geotagging, QR verification, field workflows, service delivery and programme dashboards.

  • Farmer registry and digital identity
  • GIS, geotagging and field operations
  • QR/public verification workflows
  • M&E and programme intelligence

HUMAN SERVICES · ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

HelpNova

A verified workforce and on-demand services marketplace for trusted onboarding, matching and accountable service delivery.

  • Worker/customer/company onboarding
  • Identity and guarantor verification
  • Availability/GPS matching and job lifecycle
  • Ratings, payments, wallets and earnings

HOSPITALITY · PROTOTYPE

GuestNova

A serviced-apartment and hospitality operations platform for bookings, guest profiles, service requests and facility coordination.

  • Bookings and guest profiles
  • Service requests and recurring workflows
  • Facility coordination
  • Operational and management reporting

HEALTH & COMPLIANCE · FUNCTIONAL MVP

ComplianceWatch

A regulatory licensing and compliance-monitoring platform for health facilities and personnel, with public verification and expiry-risk visibility.

  • Facility and personnel registries
  • Licence status and expiry monitoring
  • Alerts, filters and exports
  • Public licence verification

ENERGY & INFRASTRUCTURE · ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

GridLink

Digital field intelligence and renewable-energy asset management for grid operations, project delivery, energy readiness and asset performance.

  • Unified site and renewable-asset registry
  • GIS surveys and deployment monitoring
  • Incidents, maintenance and work orders
  • Energy-readiness and portfolio intelligence

EDUCATION · ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

A school attendance and education-operations platform for learner presence, teacher workflows, parent communication and accountable school operations.
