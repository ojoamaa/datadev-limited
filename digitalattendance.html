Digital Attendance

A school attendance, safeguarding and education-operations platform designed for reliable learner presence records, teacher workflows, parent communication and management visibility.

Discuss Digital Attendance →All Platforms
PLATFORM PURPOSE

Make attendance data operational, timely and accountable.

Digital Attendance is designed to replace fragmented registers with structured attendance capture and school-level workflows that can work in low-connectivity environments and support timely communication with parents and administrators.

Strategic position

Digital Attendance is one of DataDev’s six core platforms. Its education focus complements the same identity, workflow, evidence, alerts and analytics architecture used across the wider portfolio.

CORE CAPABILITIES

What Digital Attendance is designed to do.

Flexible attendance capture

QR, NFC and manual attendance workflows with architecture ready for additional approved verification methods.

Teacher workspace

Class-level attendance, learner records, exceptions and day-to-day attendance operations.

Parent communication

SMS/WhatsApp-ready alerts and attendance visibility for authorised parent or guardian workflows.

Offline-first field use

Capture designed for unreliable connectivity with controlled synchronisation when a connection becomes available.

Location & accountability

Geolocation and audit-ready records to strengthen confidence in where and when attendance activity occurred.

Management reporting

Attendance trends, exceptions, school/class views and exportable operational reporting.

OPERATING WORKFLOW

From learner arrival to actionable attendance intelligence.

Identify Learner→Capture Attendance→Validate / Sync→Alert→Monitor Exceptions→Report

Explore a school, programme or institutional pilot.
