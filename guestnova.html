GuestNova

A hospitality operations platform connecting accommodation bookings, guest services, facility workflows and management reporting.

Discuss GuestNova →All Platforms
PLATFORM PURPOSE

A focused sector solution within DataDev's shared architecture.

GuestNova is designed to give serviced-apartment and hospitality operators one structured operating layer for bookings, guest profiles, service requests, recurring facility tasks and day-to-day management visibility.

Strategic position

GuestNova is one of DataDev’s six core platforms. Its maturity label reflects current development status, not its strategic importance within the portfolio.

CORE CAPABILITIES

What GuestNova is designed to do.

Bookings & guest profiles

Structured booking records, guest details, stay information and service context.

Service requests

Guest requests routed into trackable operational workflows.

Facility coordination

Housekeeping, maintenance, utilities and recurring operating tasks.

Operational notices

Property-level rules, notices and service information for guests and staff.

Management reporting

Occupancy, service activity and operational visibility for property management.

Scalable property model

Architecture that can grow from one serviced apartment into multi-property operations.
