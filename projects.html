AGROW

Challenge: Farmer and programme data can remain fragmented across registration lists, maps, verification processes and service-delivery records, limiting field visibility and management decision-making.

DataDev response: AGROW connects farmer registration and digital identity, GIS/geotagging, QR verification, field-team workflows, service-delivery tracking, M&E dashboards and integration-ready APIs in one modular programme layer.

HelpNova

Challenge: Informal home-service and technician markets often lack consistent identity assurance, trusted worker onboarding, structured matching, service visibility and payment accountability.

DataDev response: HelpNova combines worker/customer onboarding, guarantor and identity verification, live availability/GPS matching, job lifecycle tracking, ratings, payments, wallet/earnings and company workspace functions.

WorkforceIdentityMatchingPayments
Functional MVP / demonstration build

ComplianceWatch

Challenge: Regulators need timely visibility of facility and personnel licence status, expiry risk, searchable records and a safe way for the public to verify licences.

DataDev response: ComplianceWatch provides role-based access, facility/personnel registries, active-expiring-expired monitoring, search and filters, export controls, compliance alerts and public licence verification.

GovernanceComplianceVerification
Prototype

GuestNova

Challenge: Serviced-apartment operations can become fragmented across guest bookings, service requests, facility coordination and manual operational reporting.

DataDev response: GuestNova structures bookings, guest profiles, service requests, recurring operational workflows, facility coordination and management reporting in one hospitality-operations layer.
