ABOUT DATADEV

Technology, data and programme delivery — designed around real operating needs.

DataDev Limited is a digital solutions and programme technology company focused on turning operational problems into practical systems that improve delivery, visibility and decision-making.

OUR POSITIONING

Digital infrastructure for development.

Our role is not simply to build software. We structure the data, workflows and digital architecture that programmes and organisations need to operate more effectively.

We work across field data, digital registries, GIS, monitoring and evaluation, workflow automation, analytics, service platforms and institutional systems. Each engagement starts with the operating problem and the people who must use the system.

What guides our work

1

People first

Systems must work for field teams, administrators, beneficiaries and decision-makers.

2

Data with purpose

Data collection should support action, accountability and learning.

3

Interoperable systems

We favour modular architecture and integration over unnecessary replacement.

4

Measured outcomes

Technology is judged by how well it improves programme and operational results.

DELIVERY MODEL

A programme-first implementation pathway.

We combine discovery, product design, engineering, pilot deployment and continuous improvement in one delivery approach.

Discover

Understand objectives, actors, workflows, constraints and existing systems.

Design

Define user journeys, architecture, indicators, data structures and integrations.

Build & Integrate

Develop modular components and connect them to institutional systems where required.

Pilot & Deploy

Test with real users, strengthen workflows, train teams and scale deliberately.

Measure

Track system use, programme indicators, service levels and implementation performance.

Improve

Use evidence and feedback to strengthen the product and implementation model.
