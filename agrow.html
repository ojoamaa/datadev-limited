THE PROGRAMME PROBLEM

Field programmes need one reliable operational picture.

Farmer and beneficiary data often exists in disconnected spreadsheets, partner databases, paper forms and specialised systems that do not provide a shared view of who is registered, where they are, what services they received and what changed.

AGROW is designed as a complementary digital layer that can connect registration, verification, geospatial data, service workflows and monitoring without requiring an institution to discard systems that already work.

MODULAR CAPABILITY

From registration to programme intelligence.

Modules can be deployed independently or connected through APIs to existing government, donor or private-sector systems.

Farmer Registry

Structured farmer and producer records with configurable programme fields.

GIS & Geotagging

Location capture, mapping, clustering and geographic validation.

Digital Identity

Farmer IDs, QR verification and public/private verification workflows.

Field Team Tools

Agent-oriented registration, validation and low-connectivity field workflows.

Service Integration

Readiness for advisory, input, voucher, finance and market-service connections.

M&E / MEL

Dashboards, implementation tracking, indicators and management reporting.

Open APIs

Integration patterns for external registries, platforms and institutional systems.

Data Governance

Role-based access, auditability and structured data-management controls.

Programme Analytics

Operational visibility for targeting, coverage, service delivery and learning.

VCN-RELEVANT POSITIONING

Designed to complement programme structures, not compete with them.
