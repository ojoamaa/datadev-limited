ComplianceWatch

A regulatory licensing and compliance-monitoring platform for facilities and personnel, including expiry-risk visibility and public verification.

Discuss ComplianceWatch →All Platforms
PLATFORM PURPOSE

A focused sector solution within DataDev's shared architecture.

ComplianceWatch/EdoHERMA is designed to support regulators with structured facility and personnel records, role-based access, licence-status monitoring, search/export workflows, alerts and controlled public verification.

Strategic position

ComplianceWatch is one of DataDev’s five equal-priority core platforms. Its maturity label reflects current development status, not its strategic importance within the portfolio.

CORE CAPABILITIES

What ComplianceWatch is designed to do.

Facility registry

Structured facility records with licence and compliance information.

Personnel registry

Regulated personnel records linked to status and credential information.

Licence intelligence

Active, expiring and expired classifications for operational attention.

Search & export

Filters, search and management exports for regulatory workflows.

Alerts & reminders

Notification-ready workflows for licence expiry and compliance follow-up.

Public verification

Limited public-facing verification of approved licence records.
