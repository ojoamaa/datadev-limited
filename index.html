ABOUT DATADEV

Technology is useful only when it improves how programmes work.

DataDev Limited is a technology and programme implementation company focused on building digital infrastructure for real-world development and operational challenges.

Our work sits at the intersection of people, data, institutions and delivery. We translate complex programme requirements into usable registries, dashboards, field tools, workflow systems and integrated platforms.

01

Start with the problem

Technology follows the operating need, not the other way around.

02

Design for the field

03

04

Measure what changes

A programme-first implementation model.

We combine discovery, product design, engineering and field implementation in one delivery pathway.
