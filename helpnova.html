HelpNova

A verified workforce and on-demand services platform for trusted onboarding, matching, job execution and service accountability.

Discuss HelpNova →All Platforms
PLATFORM PURPOSE

A focused sector solution within DataDev's shared architecture.

HelpNova structures the informal service journey around identity, trust, availability, assignment, evidence, payments and reputation—supporting individual workers, customers and company accounts.

Strategic position

HelpNova is one of DataDev’s six core platforms. Its maturity label reflects current development status, not its strategic importance within the portfolio.

IdentityTrustMatchingGPSJobsPayments
CORE CAPABILITIES

What HelpNova is designed to do.

Verified onboarding

Worker, customer and company registration with profile, identity and guarantor workflows.

Matching & availability

Service category, skills, availability and location-aware assignment logic.

Job lifecycle

Accept, on-my-way, arrived, start, complete and customer confirmation workflows.

Trust & reputation

Reviews, activation progress, trust scoring and service history.

Payments & wallet

Payment, wallet, earnings, withdrawal and transaction history foundations.

Company operations

Multi-staff company workspace and assignment management for institutional service delivery.

OPERATING WORKFLOW

From field or service activity to management visibility.

01

Register & verify

Worker/customer identity and required trust steps are completed.

02

Match & assign

Jobs are matched to suitable available workers or assigned by authorised staff.

03

Deliver & evidence

Job lifecycle, status updates and evidence support accountable service delivery.

04

Pay & review

Payments, wallet records and ratings close the service cycle.

05

Monitor & improve

Operational dashboards support quality, response and workforce management.
