GridLink — Digital Field Intelligence & Renewable-Energy Asset Management.

A configurable platform for grid operations, distributed-energy project delivery, renewable-asset performance, housing energy-readiness and portfolio intelligence.

PORTFOLIO POSITION

GridLink is one of DataDev’s six core platforms.

Its current maturity and sector focus are presented transparently, while strategic standing remains equal across Agriculture, Human Services, Hospitality, Health and Energy.

WHY GRIDLINK

Connect field conditions, assets and operational decisions.

GridLink extends DataDev's field-intelligence architecture into energy. It is designed to help operators, developers, government programmes and financiers see where assets are, what condition they are in, what work is required and whether delivery and performance are on track.
